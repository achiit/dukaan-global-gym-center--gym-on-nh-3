Meet the owner

Sami Ahmad

I opened Global Gym Center a year ago because our area had no proper place to train. Young people from Paprour and Bathauli were travelling far or not training at all.

So I built one on NH-31, close enough to walk or ride in. Good equipment, a clean floor, and trainers who actually watch your form.

Come in, look around, and decide for yourself. I will tell you the fee in person, no hidden charges.
